# TVW-009 — OSM building-footprint import and QA

**Status:** Luna fix 1 implementation and evidence prepared; independent Sol re-review pending. This issue covers source inspection, footprint package generation, height policy and QA only. It does not implement the TVW-010 browser building renderer or mark M4 complete.

## Scope and result

The current candidate remains the reviewed 1,000 m square around the TQ1 4HN postcode centroid, with `selection_status: selected_pending_coverage` and `coverage_status: not_checked`. The original plan is preserved unchanged; SHA-256 checked during this implementation: `144c9c704ea1c10fa2d503dba3233b089528585be7d36b7b94164d28ba40d571`.

TVW-009 downloaded and inspected a dated public Devon extract; verified the exact file bytes and PBF header; assembled source building areas before filtering; clipped local footprint geometry to the exact AOI; applied the configured height policy; and wrote an independently validated package, QA report and north-up image. Generated source intermediates and the package remain ignored and are not loaded by the browser. The synthetic world remains the default; the EA terrain route and M0 analytic route remain available.

## Measured output and QA

The current import produced **1,000** clipped building features, **1,002** polygon components and **0** interior rings in the actual AOI output. The polygons total **172,969.138 m²** (0.172969 km² per square kilometre of AOI). This density is the mapped footprint area in this extract; it is not an estimate of completeness.

Full-source tag/assembly counts and AOI processing are in `artifacts/TVW-009-buildings-qa.json`. Notable exclusions and observations:

- 17 `building=no` areas and 2,139 areas tagged only with `building:part` were excluded.
- 37 candidate areas fell outside the 50 m acquisition buffer, 192 fell outside the exact AOI after buffering, and 3 clipped footprints fell below the 1 m² minimum.
- Five tagged relations are non-area relation types rather than failed multipolygon assemblies: relation IDs `7660489`, `9226944`, `9233555`, and `9301051` have `type=building`; `7726123` has `type=site`. All five are now reported as intentionally skipped non-area relations, while true `multipolygon`/`boundary` assembly failures are **0**. The report records each relation ID/type/reason and available member-way bounds. For example, relation `7660489` references way `369356671`, whose observed WGS84 bounds are `[-3.399067, 50.754983, -3.3967888, 50.7557943]`, outside the AOI's acquisition envelope; these member locations are evidence only, not asserted footprints. None is treated as a Torquay AOI building.
- No candidate geometry was rejected as invalid; exact duplicate suppression and measured near-overlap counts were zero. Relation member ways represented by accepted relation areas are suppressed where applicable (zero in this extract).
- Heights: 998 configured 8 m visual defaults and 2 estimates from positive integer `building:levels × 3 m`; no exported building had a usable direct `height` tag. All OSM-tagged heights are treated as unverified. `roof:height` and `min_height` are retained as tags and do not change total height.

The deterministic north-up [building QA image](screenshots/TVW-009-buildings-preview.png) shows the exact 1,000 m AOI border, local metre axes, north cue, 100 m scale, centre marker, height-method summary, attribution, separated map-check IDs, zero multipolygon assembly failures, and five non-area relations skipped. It has been visually inspected after its corrected final render.
