## Suspended launch and absolute deadline

The new supervisor starts one monotonic **180-second total deadline** at full invocation entry. Preflight, imports, numerical work, cleanup and preservation share that budget; do not restart it per process. Use a numerical cutoff at absolute start+150 s, reserving up to 30 s for termination, archival and preservation. If identity work consumes the launch window, stop without numerical dispatch. An earlier stop is not a timing relaxation.

Create a noninheritable Job Object and configure only `JOB_OBJECT_LIMIT_KILL_ON_JOB_CLOSE`; no arbitrary job/process memory limit. Launch preflight/runner suspended via CreateProcessW with CREATE_NO_WINDOW and inherited file-log handles. Assign each to the Job before ResumeThread, fail closed if creation/configuration/assignment fails, and terminate an unassigned suspended process without resuming it. Do not enable breakaway. The builder's multiprocessing spawn and venv/base-interpreter descendants inherit containment. Do not let descendants inherit the Job handle itself. Close only owned handles in exception/finally paths.

Run the fixed identity-only preflight first under containment; its completion never implies permission to run a builder absent the reviewed reservation/runtime match. Then create the fixed builder suspended/assigned/resumed under the same absolute deadline. Poll with remaining-budget-clamped waits; clamp launch failure, resume failure, termination and final confirmation waits as well. At cutoff or any supervisor error, TerminateJobObject and close the job handle to enforce kill-on-close. Use finite confirmation waits and job active-process accounting to verify no numerical descendants remain. No `join()` or pipe-drain wait may extend the supervisor deadline. Redirect output to files, avoiding inherited-pipe EOF deadlocks. If termination cannot be confirmed, report it explicitly and prohibit further work/output moves until safe.

The current builder's internal 180-second worker timer remains unchanged; the external absolute cutoff takes precedence. Its normal unbounded joins/post-hashing are contained within the outer Job and killed if they exceed the external budget. Persist partial record before final cleanup. Deadline-limited or failed preservation is INCONCLUSIVE/STOP, not PASS; no wall-clock guarantee can be inferred from an uncompleted filesystem operation, and the final record must report actual observed elapsed time/overrun rather than fabricate compliance.
