OS OpenMap–Local is generalized cartography and OSM is a mapped database; labels, road symbol widths, editions, mapping dates, and the reported 2 m coordinate-operation accuracy constrain what can be concluded. These bounded comparisons cannot prove completeness or approve/freeze the AOI.
