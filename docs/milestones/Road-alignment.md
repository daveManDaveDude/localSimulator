# Road-alignment investigation

Status: focused fix round 1 implementation complete; pending independent Sol re-review. This is not TVW-016 and has not been accepted. The AOI remains `selected_pending_coverage` / `not_checked`. No renderer, source, GIS, package, UI, coordinate-transform, width, or terrain correction is recommended by these diagnostics.

## Finding

The named current 2 km road centerlines re-import exactly from the dated source into the published package after the documented 1 mm local-coordinate quantization. The largest source-to-package point delta across 98 source nodes is 0.699 mm Euclidean and 0.498 mm on either axis. This confirms internal source/package consistency; it does not establish survey-grade absolute alignment. The current road manifest reports the selected WGS84-to-BNG operation accuracy as 2 m, `best_available: false`, and the preferred 1 m OSTN15 operation's grid as unavailable.

All 463,737 road faces lie within their fixed terrain triangles in double precision. The 52,464 Float32 containment exceptions have a maximum centroid residual of 0.0539 mm, below the renderer's 1 mm drape tolerance. One Float32 triangle collapses and one reorients; the collapsed projected area is 5.82 × 10⁻¹⁰ m². These are boundary/sliver representation effects, not broad surface displacement.

Terrain profiles support a steep adjacent cross-slope beneath estimated road widths at the Pennys Hill / Teignmouth candidates. The source 1 m DTM shows comparable local values. Those samples describe terrain beneath configured ribbons; they do not establish surveyed road edges, engineered crossfall, or an incorrect centerline. The current browser scenes reproduce the named candidate areas, but the original screenshots do not establish an exact camera pose or segment match.

The 2 km package has 496,336.80 m² of per-way clipped buffers before union and priority partitioning, versus 479,504.59 m² of disjoint visible surface after group union/priority (`11.9876%` of the 4,000,000 m² AOI). The 452,412.59 m² largest connected component is a connected road network, not one road. In the registered context windows, the largest individual clipped candidate ribbons are 937.82 m² at Pennys Hill and 614.26 m² at Cricketfield / Teignmouth. This local accounting does not support interpreting network connectivity or an oblique view as one enormous road ribbon.

### Candidate-window surface accounting

These are the same registered 200 m × 200 m and 160 m × 160 m comparison-panel extents. They are local bounded accounting windows, not matches to the camera in a user screenshot. Network totals include all eligible roads intersecting each window; candidate totals include the named ways for that context. Source ribbons use the package's configured buffer parameters, AOI clipping, group unions and `vehicular > cycleway > path` priority. The comparison independently reconstructs the published footprint from the current `roads.json` `surfaceBatches[group].verticesXZ` and `indices`, clips each triangle to the local window, and unions the clipped triangles. Source visible area means AOI-clipped source polygons after priority partitioning. Published triangle-area sum adds each clipped triangle separately; published union area counts the union once. Symmetric difference is the area present in exactly one of the two footprints. Per-group and aggregate values, the source group union before priority, and the exact window coordinates are recorded in `analysis.json`.

Per-group source group-union and priority-visible areas, actual published triangle sums and unions, BNG bounds, all-way and candidate-only sums, excluded tiny ribbons, and the largest individual network ribbon are recorded under `surface.candidateWindowSummaries` in `analysis.json`. The indexed JSON coordinates describe the numeric published mesh; render-time Float32 conversion and tiny clipped boundary slivers may affect sub-millimetre-scale area comparisons. No threshold is applied. These areas do not imply surveyed width. For example, the largest network ribbon in the Arthington window belongs to Parkhurst Road (way 30482113), not to the named Arthington control.

In each per-group record, `sourceGroupUnionAreaM2` is the source ribbon union before priority, `sourcePriorityVisibleAreaM2` is the source area remaining after priority, and `publishedTriangleAreaSumM2` / `publishedUnionAreaM2` measure the actual clipped package triangles before and after union. The per-group `sourcePublishedSymmetricDifferenceAreaM2` compares that published union with source priority-visible geometry. The window aggregate fields union the source and package footprints across groups; all areas are square metres in package-local horizontal X/Z. Independent synthetic tests also prove that an equal-area translated footprint has nonzero symmetric difference and that overlapping triangles have a larger area sum than union while a gapped triangle set can have equal sum and union yet fail the intended-footprint comparison.
