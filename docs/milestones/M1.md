# M1 — Synthetic heightfield terrain

**TVW-005 status:** PASS after Luna fix 1 and independent Sol re-review. M1 is complete.

## Delivered

- A reproducible, fictional 25 × 19 Float32 terrain package at `public/worlds/synthetic-terrain-demo/`, with 2 m spacing and 48 × 36 m extent. Its deterministic generator combines a gentle slope, a Gaussian hill and a narrow ridge. The existing 3 × 3 loader fixture remains unchanged.
- A Three.js-independent terrain grid with endpoint-based coordinates, row zero north at −Z, +X east, finite/sample-count guards, exact interpolation over the renderer's fixed `(NW, SW, NE)` and `(NE, SW, SE)` triangles, inclusive edge sampling and `null` outside the grid.
- An indexed Three.js mesh with one vertex per sample, upward winding, computed normals and finite bounds. Terrain-following grid and axis cues include an N arrow directed north (−Z).
- The mesh builder rejects emitted Float32 coordinates that collapse adjacent rows/columns and rejects computed normals that are not finite unit vectors. Rejection disposes the temporary Three.js geometry and material.
- The default viewer now loads and validates the local synthetic package before creating geometry. `/?world=m0` retains the original analytic M0 demo. The HUD reports source, world, dimensions, sampled ground height, camera state, speed, FPS and boundaries; grid and wireframe controls reflect actual scene visibility/material state. Load errors show their stage and provide retry and return-to-M0 actions.
- Unit coverage for rectangular coordinates, fixed index sequence/winding, exact vertex and triangle interpolation, edges/outside handling, invalid dimensions/data, mesh normals/bounds and raycast/sample agreement. Playwright exercises M0 regression, real terrain loading/render readiness and controls, fixture-coherent ground telemetry, missing/corrupt asset errors, retry, and the return-to-M0 link.

All generated data is marked `synthetic` / `LOCAL_SYNTHETIC`, uses fictional zero-valued local origins and has no geographic coordinates. TQ1 4HN remains an unverified candidate for M2 only. No real terrain, GIS pipeline, buildings, roads or AOI data is included.
